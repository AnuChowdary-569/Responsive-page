The car in the image is a Mercedes-AMG GT, a luxury sports car crafted by Mercedes-Benz’s AMG division. AC Cobra

The car in the image is an AC Cobra, also known as the Shelby Cobra. This iconic sports car was originally produced in the 1960s as a collaboration between AC Cars of Britain and Carroll Shelby in the United States. It features a lightweight British roadster body combined with powerful American V8 engines, delivering incredible performance for its time. The design is instantly recognizable by its muscular curves, wide stance, and signature side exhausts. Modern replicas and kit versions of the Cobra remain popular among car enthusiasts for their raw driving experience and timeless design.
